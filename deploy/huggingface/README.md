# CoverWise AI

Policy understanding and decision-support assistant (not insurance, legal, financial or medical advice).
Demo uses **fictional sample policies only** - do not upload real personal policy documents to a public Space.
Add `GEMINI_API_KEY` / `GROQ_API_KEY` as Space *secrets* (optional; the app works offline without them).
